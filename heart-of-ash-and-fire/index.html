Some flames are born to destroy. Others are born to love. Riven, the Firebound champion of an ancient warrior circle, was meant to protect the realm. Instead, cursed with uncontrollable flames, he has exiled himself to the Ashen Wastes, where the only thing more dangerous than the burning sands is the power he can no longer command.

Then Evelyne arrives. A runaway scholar carrying a stolen prophecy, she names Riven as the destroyer of the realm. Riven wants her gone. Evelyne needs his protection. Neither can afford to trust the other. But when their reluctant journey awakens a mysterious bloodbound connection, Evelyne discovers that her presence can do what no spell, healer, or warrior has ever managed: calm Riven's fire.

The prophecy does not merely foretell the destruction of the realm. It demands a price. And that price may be Evelyne's own heart.

Book 2 of The Bloodbound Nine: an epic dark fantasy romance of cursed warriors, forbidden magic, ancient prophecies, and bonds powerful enough to reshape the fate of kingdoms.
