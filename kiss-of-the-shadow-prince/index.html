Kiss of the Shadow Prince

When light touches shadow, a single kiss can break two realms.

Alina Brightwind, a condemned light-wielder, is dragged to execution by the High Tribunal for crimes she never committed. In a final twist of fate, her fall into the depths beneath the world lands her in a realm where sunlight has never reached - a kingdom ruled by shadows and fear.

There, in the cold silence of a city carved from darkness, she meets Dorian Shadowborn, the exiled prince whose name is whispered with awe and dread. His power is ancient, dangerous, and bound to a curse that threatens to consume everything he rules.

Alina is a threat to his kingdom... yet also the key to its survival. Dorian needs her light to stabilize the collapsing Shadow Realm, while Alina needs his darkness to escape the hunters sent to erase her. Their alliance is fragile, fueled by necessity, suspicion, and a tension neither dares to name.

But when light brushes against shadow, something older than magic awakens - a binding that neither of them expected and neither can control. A single kiss ignites an ancient contract between realms, pulling the worlds toward a catastrophic rupture.

As the Veil weakens and creatures of forgotten nightmares rise, Alina must choose whether to trust the prince who terrifies her... or destroy the only person capable of saving her. Dorian, haunted by secrets and an exile he cannot escape, must face a truth he has buried for centuries: love might be the very force that shatters his kingdom.

In a world poised between dawn and ruin, the fate of two realms hinges on a bond forged in darkness and light - a bond that may be their salvation... or their doom.
