When a healer with forbidden magic accidentally binds herself to a cursed warrior, their bloodborne connection awakens a catastrophe older than kingdoms.

Lira has spent her life hiding the healing gift that the world fears, a magic whispered about in legends and hunted by assassins. One mistake, a desperate act of healing, ties her fate to Kael, the last heir of the House of Nightfire, a man tormented by a lethal ancestral curse and haunted by a prophecy he cannot escape.

Caught between loyalty and desire, fear and devotion, Lira and Kael must decide whether to sever the bond or surrender to it. Because some curses do not loosen their grip. They decide who survives.

Book 1 of The Bloodbound Nine: a dark romantasy of cursed mates, blood magic, destiny, and love that burns even in the coldest night.
