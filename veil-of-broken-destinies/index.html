In a world where the Veil between time and memory is fragile, nightmares from another life slip into reality. Arin Blackstar, a weary manipulator of time, and Selene Vale, a fierce wielder of fate magic, find themselves bound by echoes of a love that once ended in tragedy.

Once lovers, now sworn enemies, they are forced into a deadly dance: to mend the Veil, Arin must kill the woman he once died for, while Selene must protect the man who shattered her world. Every choice carries devastating consequences, and every memory threatens to unravel their very selves.

As ancient enemies stir, and shadows from the past claw into the present, Arin and Selene must decide: will they repeat the mistakes of their past, or risk everything to forge a future neither dared to imagine?

Bloodbound: Veil of Broken Destinies is a dark fantasy romance of tragic love, time-warping magic, and choices that can shatter - or save - the world. Perfect for fans of forbidden bonds, past-life connections, and epic magical battles entwined with heart-wrenching romance.
